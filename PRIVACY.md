# Privacy Policy — Zhongwen character sidebar

_Last updated: 18 October 2026 (site access is now opt-in)_

Zhongwen character sidebar is a Chinese-English dictionary that runs inside
your browser. It has no servers, no accounts and no advertising. Dictionary
lookups happen entirely on your device. The only data that leaves your device
is anonymous feature-usage statistics, described below, which you can turn
off.

## What the extension accesses

**Text you highlight on web pages.** The extension installs with no access to
websites. It only watches for highlighted text in a tab after you click its
toolbar icon, press its shortcut, or use its right-click menu in that tab
(Chrome's "activeTab" access, which ends when the tab loads a new page), or on
every site if you turn on "Look up highlights on every site" in the side panel
(Chrome asks you to confirm). When you then select text, the extension checks
whether it contains Chinese characters. If it does, up to 300
characters of the selection are passed to the extension's side panel so they
can be looked up. Selections without Chinese characters are ignored. The
extension does not read any other content of the pages you visit, and it does
not record which pages you visit.

## What is stored, and where

All data stays on your device:

| Data | Where | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The most recent Chinese text you highlighted | `chrome.storage.session` (in memory) | Until you clear it with the ✕ button or close the browser |
| Your Traditional/Simplified preference | `chrome.storage.local` | Until you uninstall the extension |
| Your back/forward lookup history | The side panel's memory | Until you clear it or close the side panel |
| A random usage-statistics ID (see below) | `chrome.storage.local` | Until you turn usage statistics off or uninstall |
| Your usage-statistics on/off choice | `chrome.storage.local` | Until you uninstall the extension |

The dictionary (CC-CEDICT) and the Chinese font (AR PL UKai) are bundled with
the extension, so lookups never leave your computer and no web fonts are
downloaded.

## Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics)

To learn which features are used, the extension sends anonymous usage events
to Google Analytics 4 using Google's Measurement Protocol. This is **on by
default**, and you can turn it off at any time with the **"Share anonymous
usage stats"** checkbox at the bottom of the side panel.

**What is sent:**

- A random ID generated by the extension (not linked to your name, email,
  Google account or device), a session number, and the extension's version.
- Which features you use: opening the side panel; that a lookup happened, how
  it was started (page highlight, right-click menu, search box, a link in a
  definition, and so on), which kind of view it showed, and for multi-word
  selections how many words it contained; switching Traditional/Simplified;
  pressing a speaker button (and whether an on-device or online voice was
  used); back/forward and clear; installs and updates.

**What is never sent:** the text you highlight or search for, the words you
look up, the addresses or content of web pages you visit, your browsing
history, or any personal information.

As with any internet request, Google receives your IP address when an event
is sent; Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Google
processes this data under the
[Google Analytics terms](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/terms/us/)
and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), including
[how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
Statistics are kept for Google Analytics' configured retention period and are
used only to understand and improve the extension.

**Turning it off** stops all events immediately and deletes the random ID from
your device; no "opted out" event is sent. Turning it back on creates a new,
unrelated ID.

## What is shared

Apart from the anonymous usage statistics above, the extension sends no data
anywhere. It does not sell your data or share it with anyone else, and does
not use it for any purpose other than providing and improving the
dictionary.

**Pronunciation.** When you press a speaker button, the word is spoken by
Chrome's built-in text-to-speech. The extension prefers voices installed on
your device. If your device has no Mandarin voice, Chrome may use a Google
online voice instead; in that case Chrome sends the word being spoken to
Google's speech service, under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can avoid
this by installing a Mandarin voice in your operating system's settings.

**Links.** The "CC-CEDICT" credit link opens the dictionary publisher's website
only if you click it.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Access to the current tab when you click the icon or use the right-click menu (`activeTab`, `scripting`) | To notice when you highlight Chinese text in that tab. The script only reads your selection. |
| Optional: read and change data on all websites (`<all_urls>`) | Only if you turn on "Look up highlights on every site": lets the same selection watcher run on every page without clicking the icon first. You can turn it off in the side panel or in Chrome's extension settings. |
| Side panel (`sidePanel`) | To show the dictionary beside the page. |
| Storage (`storage`) | To remember your Traditional/Simplified choice, the last highlighted text, and the usage-statistics ID and setting, as described above. |
| Text-to-speech (`tts`) | To pronounce words. |
| Context menus (`contextMenus`) | To add "Look up in Zhongwen character sidebar" to the right-click menu. |
